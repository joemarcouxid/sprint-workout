This week—
How to read this. HIGH days are all-out — sprints, jumps, heavy or fast lifting. LOW days stay genuinely easy (65–75%), because that's what makes the high days possible. Never two hard days back to back.

MED days are the one exception — intensive tempo at 80–85%, which exists because the 400 is about 70% lactic and a pure high–low week never trains that system. On any hard day, keep walking through the rests. Moving clears lactate roughly twice as fast as sitting down.

The week's shape. You pick the days off; the week is built around them. Three lift days land on your hard days, moving 8–12 reps → 5–7 → 3–5 across the week. Jumps go on the first hard day.

Racing most weekends? Change the meet date to your next race each time you finish one — the last seven days before any date you enter get the full taper.
